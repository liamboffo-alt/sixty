      How to play

      1. A new category every night. Everyone gets the same one.
      2. Hit play to reveal the category. You get 5 seconds to think, then 60 seconds on the clock, shown as 60 bulbs.
      3. Correct answers light up on the board as you type. Press Enter if one doesn’t.
      4. Small typos are forgiven. Wrong guesses cost nothing.
      5. Beat the par. Every category has a par, the score a solid player reaches in 60 seconds, so easy and hard nights are judged fairly.
      6. Come back tomorrow for the next episode and keep your streak alive.
